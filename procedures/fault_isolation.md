# Fault isolation — show-operations

**Generic training workflow.** Stop work and follow the vessel's approved safety procedures where relevant.

1. Describe the symptom precisely; record time, show phase and affected asset.
2. Establish safe operating state and protect the live performance.
3. Check recent changes, configuration version and signal/power path.
4. Isolate one component at a time using a documented test; do not bypass interlocks.
5. Compare observed behavior to expected behavior; record evidence and uncertainty.
6. Restore approved configuration or escalate; verify the fix with a repeatable test.
7. Record root cause as **unconfirmed** until the evidence supports it.

Common example categories: late readiness, cue mismatch, unresolved hazard, communications failure, schedule change.
